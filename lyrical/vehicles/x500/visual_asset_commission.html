Visual asset commission

The X500’s visual models follow the gltf-robotics workflow. Its commission, the written statement of what each part is and where its origin sits, is holybro_parts/commissions/commission-x500.yaml. Each part’s manifest, the metadata inside its <part>_visual.glb, is written from it with gltf-manifest.

Every part is modeled by Honu Robotics from the kit’s dimensions (hole patterns, tube diameters, part sizes and positions) and licensed Apache 2.0.

Datums

Every part’s origin is a named feature of the part: a hole pattern, a mounting face, the face where two parts seat. The airframe is the base part, and its datum is base_link; every other part is a component, its datum where it mounts.

The bell and the propeller share a datum, the point where the propeller seats, so both sit at the rotor slot’s origin.

Deviations from the profile

  • Partial metalness. Real metal (fasteners, standoffs, pins, laser markings on aluminum) is metallic 0.25 to 0.65, not 1: Gazebo renders full metalness black for want of an environment map. gltf-check warns on this for four parts.

  • No nominal dimension on the airframe and the battery: none of the published figures is an overall extent of the part as modeled. The commission says why for each.
